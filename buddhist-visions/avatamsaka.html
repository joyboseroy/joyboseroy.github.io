Perfect Interpenetration: Avataṃsaka

Indrajāla · Fazang's hall of mirrors · dharmadhātu

Madhyamaka asks whether an independently existing thing can be found. Huayan turns the question around and looks at what the world is like once nothing stands alone: everything contains and reflects everything else, without obstruction. Its master image is 事事無礙, "phenomena do not obstruct phenomena", the fourth of the four dharmadhātus. Fazang is said to have shown this to Empress Wu by surrounding a Buddha image and a lamp with mirrors, and by counting ten coins. Indra's net, a web of jewels that each reflect all the others, is the classic image.
